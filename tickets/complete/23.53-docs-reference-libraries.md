## What landed

14 files under `docs/reference/libraries/`: ten pages converted from the 2007 DocBook chapters, three hand-written for libraries that never had a chapter (`PGSQLDevice`, `PGSQLConnection`, `System.Platform`), and a rewritten `README.md` replacing the "Under construction" placeholder and its dead `dataphor.org` link. `docs/` goes from 437 files to 450. Twelve core-library operator pages changed one line each: the `Frontend.ExecuteScript` link that `docs-reference-operators` had to leave unlinked now resolves to `reference/libraries/Frontend.md#frontendexecutescript`.

The conversion is table-driven. `tools/docs-convert/reference-pages.tsv` gained 206 rows with no existing row touched; `reference-links.tsv` gained 32 rows and had 4 corrected; the new `library-facts.tsv` carries 55 rows of library facts read from the shipped descriptors; `reference_split.py` gained three capabilities (a `(drop)` page-map output that removes a section and its subtree, `--include` to splice a separate source in at the instruction naming it, and `--facts` to fill the *Definition* blocks) and two repairs (a declaration is looked up by the id of the nearest enclosing section, and a section title is stripped of index terms and the source's line breaks). Five tests were added, one per new branch.

Implementation detail, the departures from the ticket's assumptions and the full list of source warts carried through are in the implement handoff - commit `ticket(implement): docs-reference-libraries`. This page records the review.

## Review findings

### Reproduction - the property the rest of the chain leans on

Re-ran the split and the DocBook conversion from the committed tables into a scratch root and compared the result with `docs/` (`diff -r`): **zero differing files**. The only files under `docs/reference/` that the run does not produce are the four hand-written ones (`README.md`, `PGSQLDevice.md`, `PGSQLConnection.md`, `System.Platform.md`) plus `system-library/README.md`, which is the previous ticket's. So no generated page is hand-edited and a rerun cannot silently lose an edit. Re-ran the split after this review's own edits: byte-identical output, so the published pages still reproduce.

Also read both reports in full. The first capture was truncated - a `| head` on the pipe closed `tee` early, which hid four of the report's sections; worth knowing for the next ticket in this chain. The split reports 0 errors, 0 surviving placeholders, 95 dropped sections each with its row's reason, and 12 generated blocks removed for want of a fact. The conversion reports 352 pages, 2176 legacy links all with a target, 0 unresolved, 54 `--legacy-map` rows overriding a built-in rule and 5 matching no link - exactly the five the handoff names.

### Checked by reading and re-deriving, and correct

- **Every row of `library-facts.tsv` against the 13 `.d4l` descriptors**, by hand: versions, requisites, file names and `isassembly` flags. All correct, including the three that are easy to get wrong - `General` pins no version (`version="*"`), `PGSQLConnection` requires only `Connection` and not `System`, and `Npgsql.dll` and `Newtonsoft.Json.dll` are carried but not registered. Deriving a registered assembly's simple name from the file reference marked `isassembly` matches what `LibraryUtility.RegisterLibraryFiles` actually does.
- **The registered-class claims.** 11 `Register.cs` files: three name their classes literally (`Connection` 3, `ODBCConnection` 1, `PGSQLConnection` 1) and eight build the list by reflecting over the assembly's types. The facts table's four class rows match their source exactly.
- **`System.Platform.md`, the hand-written page making the most checkable claims**, against `libraries/System.Platform/Documents/Register.d4`: 45 declarations of 38 distinct operators, `create type FileName` constrained to 260 characters, `CreateRole('PlatformUser')`, and 46 `grant usage … to role PlatformUser` with no grant to anyone else. All correct. Same for `PGSQLDevice.md` and `PGSQLConnection.md` against their descriptors, their `Register.cs` and the device's own catalog script.
- **Every page swept** for literal placeholders, heading-level jumps and headings with nothing under them: none found. `README.md` and the three hand-written pages read end to end; `SQLDevice.md`, `SimpleDevice.md`, `System.Integration.md`, `Connection.md`, `ODBCConnection.md`, `General.md`, `Security.md` and `System.Internet.md` read in full; `Frontend.md` and `MSSQLDevice.md` read by structure with their largest sections sampled.
- **`reference-pages.tsv` loses no section.** No line was removed, so no existing row changed; and because the split errors on a section id with no row and lists rows that match nothing - it listed none - every row and every section is accounted for.

### Minor, fixed in this pass

- **`docs/reference/libraries/README.md` was wrong about eight of the thirteen pages.** Its *Registered Classes* paragraph said that where the class list is built by reflection "the page names the class-definition namespace instead". Only the two hand-written pages do; the eight converted ones carry no such section at all. Rewritten with the real counts (three list their classes, eight reflect, `General` and `Security` register nothing), and it now says where the namespace *is* readable - each library's `Source/**/Register.cs` declares it as `D4ClassDefinitionNameSpace`, and it is the library's own name for every library but the two PostgreSQL ones.
- **The same page never explained why five pages have no *Operators* section**, while its own index table promises operators for `Connection` and `System.Internet`. The 2007 chapters pulled operator descriptions in from separate files and five of those files are not in this repository; `General` and `ODBCConnection` declare no operators, but `Connection` declares one, `Security` twenty and `System.Internet` forty-four. Added a paragraph saying so and pointing at each library's own D4 as the authoritative list until a page can be generated from a running server.
- **A test picked its page-map row by position** (`pages[5]`), so reordering the fixture would have silently pointed the dropped-page assertion at a different row. It now selects the row by id.

### Tripwire parked

- **`collapse_title` in `reference_split.py` collapses only a title's own text and the text between its children, not text inside a child element**, which its docstring claimed it did. Checked every section title in `Docs2.0/` and `DocBookManuals/OperatorDocs/`: none has inline markup carrying a line break, so nothing is wrong today and the byte-identical rerun is not hiding anything. Docstring corrected and a `NOTE:` left at the site saying what to do if a later source set brings one. Not fixed, because the fix is a recursive text walk for a path no source reaches.

### Major, filed

- **`tickets/backlog/bug-reference-prose-names-unshipped-devices.md`.** Two published pages carry 2007 prose that is now false, and one of them matters: the *Settings* section of `reference/libraries/SQLDevice.md` offers "Oracle device settings" and "DB2 device settings" as plain text and has **no PostgreSQL entry**, so the page a reader goes to for "what settings does my device accept" says nothing about one of the two databases this release supports. (`SimpleDevice.md`'s *See Also* is the second, smaller instance.) Not fixable in this pass: both pages are generated, the converter can retarget a link but cannot rewrite a sentence, and a hand-edit is thrown away by the next rerun - which the two remaining conversion tickets depend on. The ticket is chained on `docs-remove-old-sources`, after which every page under `docs/` is an ordinary file, and it is scoped as a sweep of `docs/reference/` rather than a patch of these two sentences, because four more tickets will land pages from the same sources first.

### Evidence appended to an existing ticket, not filed again

- **The converted pages document fewer operators than the libraries declare**, which is the drift `docs-reference-generator` (in `tickets/plan/`) exists to find, so this is an instance of a class that already has a ticket. Measured and appended there as one bullet: `Frontend` declares 36 operators and documents 29, `SQLDevice` declares 19 and documents 6, `SimpleDevice` and `System.Integration` document all of theirs, and `Connection`, `Security` and `System.Internet` (77 declarations between them) have no operator documentation at all. The useful consequence for that ticket is that the generated-versus-published diff is the only route to a complete library page, which bears on which catalog the generator is run against.

### Checked and deliberately left alone

- **The source warts the handoff lists** - the common SQL settings table's trailing empty column, the run-together *See Also* on `System.Integration.Copy`, the example headings that name a different operator (`Frontend.Image`, `SQLDevice.SQLQuery`), `SQLDevice.AvailableTables`' example body being a sentence inside a code fence - were each verified present in the DocBook rather than introduced by the conversion. The first already carries a `NOTE:` with a revisit condition in `tools/docs-convert/README.md`; the rest are faithful carry-through of published reference text, and a hand-edit to a generated page is lost on the next rerun. No change.
- **`## Documentation` as a section heading** on `SQLDevice.md`, `MSSQLDevice.md` and `SimpleDevice.md` reads oddly on a documentation page. It is the 2007 chapter's own section title, and a `reference-links.tsv` anchor resolves against it. Left as it stands; the page map's `title` column could rename it, but renaming published reference structure is an editorial decision, not a review fix.
- **The omitted *Schema* and *Registered Classes* sections** are the right call, and are now explained on the index page in the reader's own terms. Generating them belongs to `docs-reference-generator`.
- **The defensive `DRSystemIntegrationLibrary.html` row** in `reference-links.tsv` matches no link in this source set. Kept: the built-in rule would otherwise send that name to a page that will never exist, the row costs one line, and `docs-reference-chapters` converts more chapters next.
- **No test added.** The five the implement stage added are one per new branch and each pins a fact that reaches a published page; nothing in this pass found a defect that needed a reproduction, and the gate for the conversion itself is the link checker - which is what caught the 144 broken links on the implementer's first run. The only test change is the row-selection repair above.
- **`tickets/.pre-existing-known.md` not consulted and `tickets/.pre-existing-error.md` not written:** nothing failed.

### Two figures in the implement handoff are wrong; no published page is affected

- "Nine of the eleven `Register.cs` files … reflect over every type in the assembly" - it is eight of eleven. `PGSQLConnection` names its one class literally, which is also why the index page can say three libraries do.
- "`Frontend.md` is the largest (36 operator sections under 6 categories)" - 29 operator sections under 6 categories. 36 is the number of distinct operators the library's own D4 declares, which is the drift noted above.
