## What landed

The 46 conditional-compilation directive lines for the removed browser client are gone from all 26 source files, along with the unreferenced `src/engine/Server/clientaccesspolicy.xml` and the comments that only described the removed client. 334 lines deleted, 3 added, across 29 files and one deletion, in `ticket(implement): hygiene-silverlight-conditionals`.

The property the edit had to preserve is that the symbol `SILVERLIGHT` is defined nowhere, so the C# preprocessor was already resolving every site one way. Each site kept exactly the arm the compiler was already taking and lost the directive lines and the other arm. Nothing moved and nothing was rewritten, so no compiled assembly changed and no migration note is owed.

Alongside the mechanical sweep: the one compound condition, `#if USENAMEDPIPES && !SILVERLIGHT` in `src/protocol/Common/Contracts/DataphorServiceUtility.cs`, became `#if USENAMEDPIPES` and kept that still-live switch; a `<remarks>` in `ReflectionUtility.cs` and a two-line note in `DataphorFaultUtility.cs` that described only the deleted arms were removed; and the two D4 `TODO` comments in `System.Internet` and `System.Platform` now name a client-side assembly instead of the removed client. The `Frontend.ClientTypes` row and the two `.dnt` node-type documents stay: they are published documents and a compatibility surface, and `hygiene-retire-web-silverlight-client-types` owns them.

## Gaps that remain, and why they are acceptable

The nine `src/client/DAE.Client` and `src/client/Frontend.Client` files are in no project the solution builds and in no test project's reach, so their evidence is the net-spike compile plus a site-by-site read, not a behavioural test. That is all that exists until WS6 converts them, and it is not a gap this ticket could close.

The equivalence argument is by inspection rather than by measurement: nobody compiled the tree with `SILVERLIGHT` defined before and after, because that would not build, which is the whole reason the directives were dead. The premise was re-verified instead — the symbol appears in no project file, props file, targets file, solution file or source `#define`, measured again on 2026-11-26 — and each site was read against its pre-change text.
