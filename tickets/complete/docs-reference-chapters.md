The *EDITING* shortcut table in `docs/users-guide/tutorials.md` had its last three rows repaired: two rows of both legacy sources carry a spurious fourth cell in a three-column table, which shifted every following cell and dropped the *Split Window* row off the end.

## Review findings

Everything claimed in the implement handoff was re-derived rather than read. The reproduction is exact: both commands rerun into fresh folders, every split document byte-identical, and all **354 generated pages byte-identical to the repository's `docs/`** (`diff -r` clean). Both of the handoff's headline judgement calls — not converting the shortcut appendix a second time, and not filing a ticket for `RealSQLBNF.xml` — hold up, the second more strongly than the handoff argued.

### Confirmed by re-derivation

- **The shortcut appendix really is a duplicate.** `docs/users-guide/tutorials.md` has 83 pipe-table lines in that section: 6 separators, 6 header rows, **71 data rows**. `Keys.xml` has 6 `<informaltable>`s and 77 `<row>`s, 6 of them in `<thead>` — 71 body rows. Six group headings match name for name. `DataphorSet.xml:114` attaches `&ShortcutKeys;` immediately after the Tutorials part's closing tag, which is why the AsciiDoc source carried it there. (The handoff's arithmetic — "77 pipe-table lines, six of them separator lines, 71 data rows" — silently drops the six header rows; the 71 is right.)
- **The *Type Conventions* duplicate too:** `## Type Conventions` is in `docs/developers-guide/README.md`, and `DataphorSet.xml` includes `&TypeConventions;` in both guide prefaces (lines 107 and 182). The ticket's stated reason for excluding it (GitHub cannot render the table) was indeed not the real one — it renders as HTML and GitHub shows it.
- **The three re-laid table rows are right.** `Keys.xml:143-160` puts `<entry colname="col02"><keycap>Delete</keycap></entry>` after the real shortcut cell on *Undo* and on *Redo*, in a `cols="3"` table; `Tutorials.adoc:80-84` has the same two extra cells. The restored *Split Window* row (`Ctrl+Shift+1`, "Split the editor window. Allowes two locations…") matches both sources character for character, typo included. Dropping the spurious cell is the only reading that leaves a 3-column table with 71 rows.
- **`RealSQLBNF.xml` needs no ticket, and the case is stronger than stated.** All 49 of its productions appear in `src/engine/DAE/Language.RealSQL/RealSQLParser.cs` not merely by name but **as definitions** — `<name> ::=` matches for all 49, checked programmatically. The authoritative grammar is in the source and cannot go stale, so the deletion loses nothing.
- **The two new pages reproduce their sources faithfully, typos included**, as the project's rules require: "separataed", "langauge" and the non-optional `<between clause>` are in `RealSQLLanguageGuide.xml`; the unterminated quote at `ProgrammingTopics.xml:43`, "ommitted" and "Repeates" are in `ProgrammingTopics.xml`.
- **The `var` contradiction on the RealSQL page is real.** `RealSQLParser.cs:1023` (`ActualParameter`) switches on `Keywords.Var` and builds `new ParameterExpression(Modifier.Var, …)`, against the page's sentence that variable arguments "may not be invoked". Correctly filed as an arm on `bug-reference-prose-names-unshipped-devices` rather than hand-edited into a generated page.
- **The three new `reference-links.tsv` rows are the right target.** `docs/developers-guide/d4-table-operators.md#aggregate` has no per-operator subsection — it documents the `group … add` clause — so `D4LGTableExpressions-Aggregate-Count/Max/Min.html`, whose link text is the operator name, belongs on the operator's own reference page. The alternative would land the reader on the wrong operator.
- **Every file count in the accounting table is exact**: 59 XML and 172 other files in `Docs2.0/`; 79 top-level files (70 XML) plus 4 `Templates/`, 80 `images/` and 275 `OperatorDocs/` in `DocBookManuals/`. Enumerating all 59 `Docs2.0` names against the table leaves nothing unclassified, and the absent `UsersGuidePart2.xml` is absent because no such file exists. The 108 image stems resolve too, once `Images/Working/` is recognised as a directory and `Dataphor Logo.gif` as the renamed `docs/images/dataphor-logo.png`; `Corel Export notes.txt` is the only stem with no counterpart.
- **`UsersGuidePart3.xml` is one paragraph over three chapters whose every body is an `<?xm-replace_text?>` placeholder** — not worth a page, as the handoff said.
- **Gates.** `python -m unittest discover -s tools/docs-convert` 29 tests OK; `check_links.py docs --pending …` 452 files / 3400 links / **0 errors**, 2 pending (both pre-existing and unrelated); `check_size.py` 13 files, 0 over; `dotnet build Dataphor.slnx` 0 errors, 127 warnings. Accepted: the alternative is editing two legacy sources that are the record of what was published, and that conversion is not rerun by this chain. Already a `NOTE:` in `tools/docs-convert/README.md`.
- **`feat-revive-dropped-reference-documents` as one ticket with five arms** rather than five tickets. Left as one: it is one triage decision against one deadline, framed as `feat-dac-developers-guide` was for the same tree.
